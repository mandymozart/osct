Open decisions – my recommendation in gold

  1. "Always for YouTube" at all, or ask every time? → offer it, unticked, revocable in Settings (C) – newspapers do the same; asking every time gets tiring in a book full of links.
  2. Other web pages a Links entry embeds (today an iframe of the whole page, also loaded at once) – gate them too? → yes, same card ("Page on example.org – opening loads the page from example.org, which may set cookies…", button "Show page"), one "Always" per site.
  3. Poster: A (plain black) or A′ (the entry's own image behind)? → A′ when the entry has an image, else A.
  4. Texts are placeholders – the final wording (privacy notice) with whoever writes the app's privacy page; EN + the other reader languages via i18n.

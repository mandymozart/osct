#355 concept 2 – the landing needs two choices: a scene and a surface

No landing page editor, no route of its own, no slots to fill by hand. The experience's settings (⚙ in the top bar) get one small section. The landing plays the chosen surface as its hero – its picture, its targets and their AR content – and takes everything else from what the experience already has.

The same engine the editor's stage uses renders the surface: its reference picture as the paper, every target on it shown as found, entities playing in a loop, the camera drifting slowly. Reduced motion or no WebGL: the surface's picture, still.

3 · In the content
# experience.yaml – optional
landing:
  scene: studio-a
  surface: surface

Two ids. A minor content-schema step; older apps ignore it. A deleted scene or surface: the default again, and the editor says so in the section.

Comes from the experience – nothing to set
  • Title, author, publisher → the Texts above
  • Hero → the chosen surface
  • Texts ("Get the app", the three steps) → the app's, in the visitor's language
  • QR code and address → the experience's own path
  • Footer link → Privacy & terms (#353)
Left out on purpose
  • Picked images, own landing texts – gone from concept 1
  • Shop items: stay in the app's configuration for OSCT → own small issue when a second experience needs a shop
  • Publisher address / privacy additions per experience → with the white label (#347); the page keeps Building Fictions until then
  • Before the app has loaded, the static landing shows the surface's picture; the animation starts once the engine is there

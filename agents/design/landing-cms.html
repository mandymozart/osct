Open decisions – my recommendation underlined
  1. Where in the editor → Experience → a "Landing" tab next to Texts and Appearance, the stage previewing the landing (frame 1).
  2. Texts per language? The experience's other texts (title, entries) have one language → one language here too, empty = the app's translated text. Translations of content come with the content's languages, not here.
  3. The privacy page → platform part fixed and translated, the experience adds its publisher and optional additions (frame 3) – a white-label customer should not have to write the GDPR part, and cannot make it wrong.
  4. Governing law → Netherlands only for now (the platform runs from Amsterdam); a choice of law per publisher once a customer needs it.
  5. The static landing for other experiences (frame 5) → keep today's split, server-rendered per experience with the white label.
  6. Order → after #354 lands: schema + reader first (fixture, the reader reads `landing` / `publisher`), then the editor tab, then the MCP tools (put_experience_landing). The hero's scene slot waits for #354's layout.

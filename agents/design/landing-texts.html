Decisions – my recommendation underlined
  1. Languages. The landing shows the visitor's language today. → Texts per language, EN DE FR NL as a switch above the fields; each empty field shows the app's text for that language. Alternative: one set of texts for every visitor (simpler, but a German visitor then reads English where you typed English).
  2. Which texts. → Intro, Get the app, the QR line, the shop heading. The QR code's alt text, "Scan with your phone" and "Privacy & terms" stay the app's (accessibility and the legal link should not drift).
  3. Shop. → Up to 4 items, each a label, a link and a picture (from the Library, or the book's cover as today). Labels per language? → no, one label – product names rarely translate. An experience without items keeps today's two placeholders until someone edits the list; an emptied list removes the shop section.
  4. Stored → in the experience (landing.texts, landing.shop), published in landing.json – export, versions and publish carry it, like the hero.

#356 – one workspace shell: the stage is the window, and it is not always 3D

Adopted from SCUTOONS' creator: the stage is the whole window, the address decides what it shows, panels float over it and never push it, a workspace is a preset of panels and tabs. OSCT's scene editor already works like this – but only there, with the 3D engine built into the page. Lifted out, the same shell carries the Experiences page and the landing page (#355), each with its own stage.

A panel tab is declared once (name, icon, about) and filled by the workspace that has content for it – a tab without content is not offered. Layouts stay remembered per workspace in the browser, as today. landing becomes a reserved scene id.

Decisions – my recommendation underlined
  1. The Experiences page: SCUTOONS shows its dashboard as an overlay over a dimmed stage. → Here a workspace of its own (frame 1): OSCT has no "empty stage" to dim, and you asked for a stage.
  2. What the landing lets you edit → hero (scene, surface), shop items, publisher (frame 2). Its texts stay the app's translations.
  3. Where the landing content is stored → in the experience (landing, publisher – a minor content-schema step), so export, versions and publish carry it.
  4. Order → (a) lift the shell out of the scene page, nothing visible changes · (b) the landing workspace + its content fields (#355) · (c) the Experiences page into the shell. (a) is the risky part: the scene page is 1 200 lines with the engine wired in.

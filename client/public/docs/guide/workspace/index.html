Workspace and panels

The stage fills the window and the panels float over it. One selection is shared by the tree, the stage and the Inspector.

Panels and tabs

History, LogicclosedOpen them from the panels menu (History also from the clock in the top bar).
  • Move a panel by its head. It stays where you drop it, over another panel too, and never leaves the stage. Reset layout in the panels menu puts every panel back.
  • Resize by its edges; the panels in the way make room. Double-click an edge for its own size.
  • Fold a panel to its head with a double-click on the head. Close with ×.
  • Drag a tab into another panel's head, or onto the stage to make a panel of its own.
  • The panels menu (top bar, right) lists every tab, brings closed ones back and has Reset layout.

The layout is remembered per browser. The Inspector can be folded but never closed.

An experience has a second workspace: Landing in the top bar, next to Scenes, shows its landing page with the same kind of panels. The Experiences page works the same way: the books on the stage, the Inspector and Info over them – each workspace remembers its own layout.

The tree

From top to bottom: the scene, its surfaces, then each entry with its target and the target's entity as a tree of nodes.

  • A click on any row selects it.
  • The eye switches a target off and on, as if the camera lost and found the page.
  • The footer stays at the panel's foot: + add, and for the selected row duplicate (a node with its children; the copy's names get a free number, door → door 2), group (the node into a new group in its place) and remove.
  • + add opens one menu of everything the scene can get: a surface, a target, an entry, or a node of any type onto the selected target or node (greyed out without one). Type to filter; what you add is selected.
  • Right click a row for its menu: remove, move up or down, into or out of a node.
  • Drag a node row onto another to make it a child, or between rows to reorder.
  • Drag a target row onto a surface row to move it to that surface.

Keys: ↑ ↓ walk the rows, ← → fold and unfold, Alt+↑ ↓ reorder a node, Tab / Shift+Tab indent and outdent, Delete removes the selected node (on a surface, its Inspector asks first).

Search

The top bar's search finds entries (title and text), scenes, targets and named nodes across the whole experience. A pick opens its scene and selects it. / or Ctrl+K focuses the field.

Autosave

An edit shows on the stage at once and is saved about a second after you stop. The top bar says where the draft stands. Not saved names what keeps it back, e.g. a rule that is not complete yet.

Undo edits at the foot of a panel takes its subject back to how it was when you opened the page.

Undo and History

  • ↶ ↷ in the top bar, or Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z: your own saved changes since the page opened, one step at a time.
  • History (the clock): every saved change of the experience, newest first, by anyone, with filters for scene, kind and person.
  • Click a row for Revert (that change) or Jump back to here (everything after it).

A revert changes the draft only. Readers see it with the next publish. Plugins are in it too: a save, a push from someone's folder, a file deleted in the Code tab, a whole plugin – each can be undone.

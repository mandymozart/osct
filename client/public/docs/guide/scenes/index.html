Scenes and surfaces

An experience is an ordered list of scenes. A scene has one or more surfaces, and the targets lie on them. There are no page numbers: a scene has a label.

The Scenes panel

The count is of the targets that are switched on – the ones the reader tracks, at most 10 per scene. Switched-off targets are named next to it (3 of 10 · 13 off).

Groups

A group is a label over neighbouring scenes: one stop in the reader's switcher. In the panel it is a band over its cards. Click the band to select the group and type its label in the Inspector.

The band shows the group's target count. It turns into a warning when the group holds more targets than the app keeps loaded at once.

Surfaces

The tree lists the scene's surfaces under Surfaces. Its + adds a double page, single page, wall or custom one and selects it, so the Inspector shows it at once. A surface row's menu (right click) moves it up or down and removes it. Arrange in a row (the scene's Inspector, or the scene row's menu) lays them flat side by side.

Select a surface (its row in the tree, or the Surface tool, S) to edit:

  • Label, format and size in mm. Targets that end up outside are named in red.
  • Place: position and rotation in the scene. The Surface tool's gizmo does the same on the stage.
  • Grid: the surface's own grid for its targets.
  • Reference image: a picture of the page or wall, shown on the stage to place targets against. It is an editing aid; readers never get it.

A surface with entries or targets on it is removed only after a confirmation that names them. They fall back to the scene's first surface.

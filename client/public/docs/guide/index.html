Editor guide

The editor at a glance

The editor runs the same engine as the reader app, over a simulated page instead of a camera picture. What you place on the stage is what a reader sees on the printed page.

The words

WordWhat it is
ExperienceThe whole work: a book, a wall, a room. It has a title, scenes, texts, files and code.
SceneOne step of the experience, e.g. a double page. The reader's switcher steps through the scenes in their order.
GroupSeveral scenes under one label: one stop in the reader's switcher.
SurfaceA physical area of a scene with a size in mm: a double page, a single page, a wall.
TargetA printed image the camera recognises. It lies on a surface at a place in mm. At most 10 per scene.
EntryThe text a reader unlocks and reads: title, category, body. Usually it belongs to a target; it can also stand alone.
EntityThe AR content on a target: a tree of nodes.
NodeOne thing in that tree: a group, model, video, image, text or light, or a type a plugin adds.
ComponentSomething a node does: an animation, a behaviour (rules), a plugin's code.
PluginThe experience's own code: components, node types, materials, passes, looks.
Landing pageWhat a computer gets instead of the app: the book, its 3D hero, a QR code for the phone and the shop.
DraftWhat the editor saves. Readers never see it.
PublishWrites the draft as a version readers get.

The pages

  • Experiences (/editor/): the dashboard – every experience of this installation, where you worked last, making, importing, rolling back and deleting one, and connecting an AI agent. More
  • An experience (/editor/<experience>/): opens the scene you had open last, else the first.
  • The workspace (/editor/<experience>/<scene>/): the stage with the panels around it. This is where the work happens. More
  • The landing workspace (/editor/<experience>/landing/): the experience's landing page with the draft, and its hero. Scenes · Landing in the top bar switches between the two. More

Admins also have /editor/admin (short: /admin) for users, roles and QR codes. More

Signing in

You need an account with the role editor. Enter your e-mail and type the code that arrives by mail, or use I have a password. An admin gives the role on the admin page. The sign-in is the site's: signing in or out in the reader app does the same here.

The editor is made for a desktop and an iPad. There is no phone layout.

Saving and publishing

  • Every edit saves itself to the draft, about a second after you stop. There is no Save button. The top bar says Saving…, Saved, or Not saved with the reason.
  • The Code tab is the exception: code is saved with Save (Ctrl+S), so half-typed code never reaches the draft.
  • Readers get nothing before Publish. The top bar shows draft only or live on ….
  • Undo works on saved changes: Ctrl+Z, or the History panel for older ones.

Help inside the editor

The Info panel (bottom left) names and explains whatever control is under the pointer or has the focus. Open it when a button's meaning is not clear.

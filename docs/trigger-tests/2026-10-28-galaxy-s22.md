## Analysis

**Size is the clearest factor.** Every miss is printed at 10.8 cm² or less. Of the larger triggers almost all were
found:

| Printed area | Found |
|---|---|
| under 6 cm² | 5 of 9 |
| 6–8 cm² | 23 of 34 |
| 8–10 cm² | 22 of 30 |
| 10–15 cm² | 43 of 45 |
| 15 cm² and more | 3 of 3 |

**The image content matters, not where the image comes from.** Link screenshots (`OSCT_Ref_*`) were found 32 of
41 times and print images (`P…_Print_*`) 64 of 80 times, 78 % and 80 %. What decides is how much texture an
image has, because the engine recognises an image by its corner points. Measured on every target image, scaled to
320 px (corner points, and the share of the image without edges):

| Target | Image | Corner points | Flat area | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| p067-1 | Blender logo | 4 | 85 % | not found |
| p129-1 | Open source logo (half circle) | 6 | 89 % | not found |
| fewest of the found ones | p188-1, p082-1, p068-1 | 25–27 | 73–77 % | found |
| median of all 121 | | 134 | 37 % | |

The two logos have four to six times fewer corner points than any image that was found. Flat graphics with clean
shapes do not work as triggers, at any size or focus. They need a different image, or more texture in the design.

Three misses have many corner points but repeat the same shape across the image, which makes the points
ambiguous for matching: p101-1 (Muybridge's grid of horse photos), p096-1 (blackletter lettering) and p176-1 (a grid
of interface icons). That is a suspicion, not measured yet.

The other misses have as much texture as the found ones (median 144 corner points against 133). For them, the size and the
focus remain the likely cause. The measurement of all 121 images is in `target-texture.txt` next to the photos.

**Focus.** During the test the camera seemed not to focus. Small triggers have to be held close for the engine to
see enough of them, and that is where focus fails first. What the app asks of the camera today:

- **Lens:** the 8th Wall engine picks the back camera itself. On Samsung phones with more than two cameras it
  chooses the one labelled `camera2 0, facing back` (the main camera), otherwise the browser's default back camera.
- **Resolution:** the engine asks for exactly 960 × 720 first and gets it on most phones.
- **Focus:** neither the app nor the engine sets a focus mode, focus distance or zoom. The browser's default
  applies, which on Android Chrome is usually continuous autofocus.
- A phone's main camera has a minimum focus distance. Closer than that the picture stays blurry whatever the
  autofocus does. Phone camera apps switch to another lens for close-ups; a web page cannot.

Not measured yet: which lens and focus mode the S22 actually used, and from what distance the small triggers were
tried. Since 1.8.0 the debug overlay and the trial panel show the camera (lens, resolution, focus mode, distance and
range, zoom range) and have test switches for continuous focus, tap to focus and zoom (`docs/tracking.md` "Camera"),
so small triggers can be scanned from further away. The trial report records the camera.
